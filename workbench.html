Critical finding: the direct_sales discount is not derivable from Zoho

22 of 42 fixtures (sales_channel: "direct_sales") apply a flat 10% discount on the hand-made invoice with zero trace of it anywhere in the corresponding Zoho sales order — no discount field, no custom field, no note. The other 20 (sales_channel: "shopify") do carry it in Zoho's own data, reliably.

User decision (2026-08-20): park every non-Shopify-channel order for human review rather than hardcode an inferred 10% rule — matches SPEC.md §2's "no silent partial success" gate literally. Consequence: roughly half the golden corpus cannot produce a generated invoice at all; for those fixtures Bar A is graded as "did it correctly refuse and park," not "did it win the blind A/B." Full writeup: fixtures/golden/COVERAGE_NOTES.md and CONTRACT.md.

Also found: fixture 016-leticia-azevedo is a genuine human double-discount error (its invoice total doesn't reconcile to its own sales order). The mapping code is being built to produce the mathematically correct total, not to replicate that bug.

Round 0 decisions

  • Runtime: Node.js + TypeScript + Express + better-sqlite3, single self-contained process. User-approved.
  • Bar E interface: server-rendered dashboard at / (status counts + order table) and /orders/:id (full request/response log, correlation ID, replay button), plus a JSON mirror at /api/orders.
  • Idempotency enforced at the storage layer: orders.zoho_salesorder_id is UNIQUE; concurrent duplicate webhook deliveries race safely to a single row.
  • Secrets never reach storage: redact.ts strips known secret env values from every logged URL/header/body before it's written; unit-tested.
